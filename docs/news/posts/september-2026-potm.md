## About Grow, Seed! Pinball

Sid did not want to fall to the bottom. Help our little friend grow up without getting buried! In Grow, Seed! Pinball you will:

* **Keep Sid bouncing** through the pinball field!
* **Help him grow upwards** while avoiding being buried.
* Put your pinball skills to the test in this quirky little game created for the **Ludum Dare 34** competition.
* **Master your shots** and keep Sid alive as long as you can!
