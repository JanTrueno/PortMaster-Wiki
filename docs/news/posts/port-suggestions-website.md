# Introducing the Port Suggestion website

We are proud to present a new addition to PortMaster: a central hub designed to
harness the collective creativity and insights of our community.

<!-- more -->

This platform lets you suggest and rate potential game ports, directly
influencing our next projects.

## Features

* **Interactive rating system.** Vote on your favourite game suggestions.
* **Submission portal.** Add your own port suggestions.
* **Edit and view options.** Manage your existing suggestions with ease.
* **Moderated submissions.** Quality and relevance checked by our moderators.

## How it works

Each game is tagged with a feasibility score of High, Medium or Low, indicating
how easy it would be to port. Accuracy matters, so do your research before
suggesting a game.

Before you suggest something, use the search feature to check it isn't already
there, check the game against what can realistically be ported, and familiarise
yourself with the platform's limitations.
