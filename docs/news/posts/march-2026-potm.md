## About orbo

orbo is a minimalist single-button challenge where:

* You **move using orbit-based momentum** instead of traditional controls!
* **Precision and timing are everything** as you swing around obstacles.
* **Simple mechanics quickly turn into a test** of skill, patience and mastery!

Easy to learn, tough to perfect. Every movement counts in this elegant physics-based experience.
