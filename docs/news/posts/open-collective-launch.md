# We have launched an Open Collective

Big news, PortMaster family. We have just kicked off our Open Collective.

<!-- more -->

This is a big step for us to keep growing and bringing more to the table. Think
more games, better support for devices, and eventually some upgrades to our
build setup.

Everything is transparent, just like we like it. You can see where every penny
goes.

If you feel like chipping in, awesome. If not, no sweat, your energy and
enthusiasm are what really keep us going.
