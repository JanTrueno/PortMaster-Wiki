# Our first Port of the Month: ROTA

We are thrilled to introduce our first ever Port of the Month: **ROTA**!

<!-- more -->

![ROTA Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PortsMaster/PortMaster-New/main/ports/rota/screenshot.jpg)

## About ROTA

ROTA is an innovative game where you:

* **Rotate gravity** as you walk over the edge!
* **Push, pull and rotate gravity-blocks** to navigate and solve intricate
  puzzles.
* **Collect all 50 gems** to unlock doors and explore 8 vibrant worlds!
* Enjoy an **original ambient soundtrack** that keeps you relaxed while tackling
  challenging puzzles.
* Watch out for spikes!

## Port of the Month Rules

You can either **participate** or **compete**!

### To Participate
Share a photo of your device, your name, and the date of completion to earn the
**@POTM Participant** role.

### To Compete
Run the game in speedrun mode. Go to Options, then under Speedrun, change Clock
to BOTH. Post your final time with your name and date of completion. The fastest
time during the month earns the **@POTM Champion** role.
