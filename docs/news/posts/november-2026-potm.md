## About Rolling Bird

Rolling Bird is a retro arcade shooter, heavily inspired by Namco's 1986 classic **Rolling Thunder**. Infiltrate the enemy hideout, survive the onslaught, and rack up the highest score you can. In Rolling Bird you will:

* **Enjoy fast-paced arcade action** from the first second.
* **Take on waves of enemies** that keep on coming.
* **Rely on tight, responsive controls** to dodge, shoot and stay alive.
* **Chase the high score**, with plenty of opportunities to turn a great run into a very short one!
