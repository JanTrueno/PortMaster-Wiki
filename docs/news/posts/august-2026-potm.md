## About 2048 Plus

2048 Plus is a polished take on the classic sliding tile puzzle.

* **Merge matching numbers** and plan your moves carefully.
* **See just how high you can climb.**
* It starts off simple, but **reaching the biggest tiles is a real challenge**!
