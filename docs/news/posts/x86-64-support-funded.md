# Bringing PortMaster to x86_64

We are setting our sights on x86_64 devices, and thanks to the community, the
development unit needed to do it has been funded.

<!-- more -->

We are gearing up to add PortMaster support to x86 devices. This move will not
only broaden our device compatibility but also improve the experience for people
using custom firmware like RetroDECK and Batocera.

Bringing PortMaster to the Steam Deck and similar devices means offering an
experience that goes beyond mainstream gaming. It opens up a world of
possibilities, letting our community discover exceptional indie titles that are
often overshadowed by bigger names.

To kickstart this project we aimed to secure a development unit for our lead
developer kloptops. The goal has been reached and the device has been ordered.

> everyone.

Thank you for being such an incredible community.
