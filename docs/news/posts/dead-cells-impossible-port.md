# The Anatomy of an Impossible Port

The awesome @dash from The Bryant Review did a great write up of the work the
amazingly talented @bmdhacks did in bringing **Dead Cells** to the R36S and
other devices.

<!-- more -->

It really is a work of epic proportions. Months of work, and a truly epic dev
log of the hurdles that had to be overcome to even make this impossible port
possible on the lowest of low devices.

On the PortMaster suggestions page, Dead Cells sat for a long time with 178
votes and a feasibility rating of "Low". It wasn't a dismissal, but it was a
feeling that everyone seemed to share. The community wanted it. They just
didn't believe it could ever happen.

We wouldn't normally push an article this hard, but this one is worth it.
