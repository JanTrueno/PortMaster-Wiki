# New Collective goal: 2x Trimui Smart Pro S

A new Open Collective goal has been added: **two Trimui Smart Pro S units**.

<!-- more -->

The new Trimui Smart Pro S has a new chipset that has some compatibility issues
with our ports under KNULLI, and we would like to help resolve these. That way
TSP S owners can play our beloved PortMaster ports the way they are meant to be
played.

We are seeking funding for two development units for game testing and support.

Thank you everyone for your support!
