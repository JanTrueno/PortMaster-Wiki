## About Dragon Dragon Fire Fire

Dragon Dragon Fire Fire is a fast-paced arcade platformer where you:

* **Play as a fire-breathing dragon** blasting through destructible levels in a late-80s arcade style!
* **Incinerate enemies and terrain** with blazing fire breath as you chase the high score!
* **Enjoy fast-paced platforming chaos** that keeps the action intense and addictive!

It's like if classic arcade spirit and fiery dragon fury had a pixelated love child—and your reflexes are the key to survival!
