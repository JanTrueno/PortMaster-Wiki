# On unlicensed port files in stock firmware

We need to address a serious issue. A new stock OS image has been released that
includes unlicensed port files for several games. This goes against the
principles of fair use and respect for intellectual property.

<!-- more -->

## Our stance

**These ports are not supported by PortMaster.** We cannot and will not provide
any assistance or troubleshooting for these files.

At PortMaster we believe in supporting game developers and publishers by
ensuring that all game ports are created and used with legitimately purchased
software. Our platform lets you install ports for games you have bought on
trusted platforms like Steam, GOG and itch.io. This approach ensures developers
receive the compensation they deserve, which encourages further innovation and
quality.

## Why this matters

Sharing unlicensed copies not only undermines the hard work of game developers,
it also threatens the integrity of projects like PortMaster. Our community
thrives on respect for intellectual property and fair use, and any behaviour
that compromises those values cannot be tolerated.

## What we are doing

* **Website and app banners**, to clearly distance ourselves from these
  unauthorised ports and reinforce our commitment to legitimate software use.
* **A protest initiative.** We will redirect our homepage to a statement page
  for a day, explaining the situation and urging users to support legitimate
  software use.

We urge all members of our community to stand with us. By using and sharing only
licensed software, we help maintain a healthy ecosystem that supports developers
and ensures the longevity of great gaming experiences.

Thank you for your understanding.

*The PortMaster Team*
