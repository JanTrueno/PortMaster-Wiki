# Retroid Pocket 5 development units funded

We have reached our funding goal for the Retroid Pocket 5, which will feature
Linux custom firmware for the first time.

<!-- more -->

To bring the PortMaster ports you know and love to this device, we aimed to
secure three development units so we could ensure smooth compatibility and
performance. The goal of 600 euro has been met.

A huge thank you to everyone who contributed. Your support made this possible
and we could not have done it without you.

We will keep you posted with any future updates, and once again, thank you for
helping us reach this milestone.

*The PortMaster Team*
